Stephen Cheng

I am a 2nd year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Maryland advised by Sarah Wiegreffe. My current research interests lie in interpreting and improving post training optimization methods for LLMs. I am also interested in mechanistic interpretability and alternative model architecture/pre-training paradigms.

In the past, I have also collaborated with Dinesh Manocha. During my MS in Computer Science at Northwestern University, I worked with Han Liu on robot learning and time series forecasting. During my BS in Electrical Engineering, I worked with John Rogers on optogenetics, and Hooman Mohseni on thin-film memristors.

In my free time, I enjoy going to the gym, cooking/baking, and music concerts.
